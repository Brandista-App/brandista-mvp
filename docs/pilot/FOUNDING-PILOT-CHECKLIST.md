# Founding Brand Pilot Checklist

Complete this with the project owner and partner before customer-facing pilot activity. These are operational checks, not statements that an agreement or partner already exists.

## Partner and scope

- [ ] Written agreement on pilot scope, duration, included products, and review contacts.
- [ ] Brand application reviewed; authorized operator records the outcome.
- [ ] Brand contact person and escalation contact confirmed.
- [ ] Brand name, public copy, and storefront presentation approved by the partner.
- [ ] No one represents the brand as legally verified solely because its application was approved.

## Catalogue and rights

- [ ] Product names, descriptions, categories, and agreed PKR prices confirmed by the partner.
- [ ] Product availability and stock-check responsibility are clear; current software tracks only available/unavailable, not quantities.
- [ ] Image rights and permissions are documented before any image is displayed elsewhere; current API has no image storage.
- [ ] Each product is reviewed in draft and explicitly published by an authorized operator.
- [ ] Public brand listing, product details, displayed price, availability, and cart behavior are checked.

## Operations and customer commitments

- [ ] Order fulfilment responsibility and service expectations agreed in writing.
- [ ] Shipping regions, fees, and delivery estimates agreed and accurately disclosed.
- [ ] Returns, cancellations, refunds, and complaint handling owners agreed.
- [ ] Customer support contact and response ownership agreed.
- [ ] Payment behavior explained accurately; live payment and order placement are not implemented.
- [ ] Data privacy, contact-detail access, retention, and access permissions reviewed by the project owner.
- [ ] Pilot feedback method, incident process, and success measures agreed.
- [ ] Pause/removal procedure and authorized operators are known to both parties.

## Go / no-go

- [ ] Staging environment is available and readiness checked, or pilot is explicitly limited to a safe local/internal rehearsal.
- [ ] No production credentials or real customer data are present in demo/test setup.
- [ ] Owner has approved proceeding with the specific pilot scope.
